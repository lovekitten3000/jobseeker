## Job Description

Blacktown is one of Australia's most diverse communities. Our Community
Development team works with residents, community groups and service providers
to strengthen inclusion, participation and community wellbeing across the city.

As Community Development Officer – Multicultural, you lead the delivery of
Council's Multicultural Strategy and Action Plan. You research community needs,
analyse demographic trends, meet with residents and service providers, and
provide advice that helps Council respond to the priorities of culturally and
linguistically diverse communities.

You support the Multicultural Advisory Committee, coordinate community projects
and events, and help make Council information, facilities and services more
accessible and equitable.

You build partnerships with local organisations, strengthen connections across
the service sector, and contribute to social planning through community
consultation, data collection and analysis.

You work closely with colleagues across the Community Development team to
deliver practical outcomes that improve the social, cultural and physical
wellbeing of Blacktown residents.

Every day offers opportunities to grow through training, mentoring and hands-on
experience. Your work helps people feel included, connected and supported, and
your contribution makes a visible difference in the community.

If you are passionate about strengthening communities, building partnerships
and delivering practical outcomes that matter, we would love to hear from you.

At Blacktown City Council, Good People Make Great Teams, And Great Teams Build
Great Cities. Here, You Will Find The Stability And Support To Keep Growing,
Along With:

* a secure, permanent, full-time role with one of Australia's largest councils
* an entry level salary commencing at $1,940.24 per week
* a 35-hour working week
* a car allowance
* access to Council's flextime arrangement
* 12% superannuation and competitive leave entitlements
* ongoing training and development support
* access to Fitness Passport, social clubs and wellbeing programs.
