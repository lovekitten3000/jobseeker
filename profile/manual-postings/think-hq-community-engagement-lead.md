# Think HQ — Community Engagement Lead

## Job Description

We’re looking for a Community Engagement Lead to help deliver inclusive,
best-practice engagement that meaningfully connects with communities across
Australia. This is a hands-on, relationship-driven role within our supportive
Audience Insights & Engagement team.

The role can be based in either Melbourne (South Melbourne) or Sydney (Surry
Hills), with our focus firmly on finding the right person for the role and team.

### About the role

As Community Engagement Lead, you'll support the delivery of community
engagement initiatives that are respectful, thoughtful and outcomes-focused.
Working alongside senior engagement practitioners, you'll help build and
maintain trusted relationships with a wide range of communities, stakeholders
and organisations.

You'll work closely with community leaders, service providers and advocacy
organisations to coordinate engagement activities across a range of client
projects. From consultations and focus groups through to case studies,
workshops and community events, you'll help ensure community voices are
represented authentically and respectfully.

This is a role for someone who enjoys working with people, thrives on
organisation and coordination, and values building genuine, trust-based
relationships. You'll be equally comfortable out in the community,
coordinating logistics behind the scenes, or working alongside project teams
to deliver high-quality engagement.

Key responsibilities include:

**Community engagement and delivery**

* Build and maintain positive relationships with community stakeholders, organisations and networks.
* Support the planning and delivery of community engagement activities across multiple projects.
* Recruit and coordinate participants for consultations, interviews, focus groups and workshops.
* Coordinate case studies, community ambassadors and subject matter experts.
* Assist with facilitating community discussions and engagement activities.
* Help ensure engagement activities are culturally responsive, inclusive and well organised.

**Project coordination**

* Coordinate engagement activities across client projects, ensuring timelines and deliverables are met.
* Prepare stakeholder lists, contact databases and engagement materials.
* Manage schedules, logistics and communications for engagement activities and events.
* Maintain accurate CRM records, participant databases and project documentation.
* Support reporting, evaluation and project administration.

**Team and client support**

* Work collaboratively with project teams to deliver engagement activities to a high standard.
* Share community insights and observations with the broader project team.
* Support proposal development and other business development activities where required.
* Contribute to continuous improvement of engagement processes, resources and ways of working.

### About you

We’re looking for someone with the drive, curiosity and capability to grow into
the role. You don’t need to have mastered everything already, but you’ll likely
bring:

* Around 2-4 years' experience in community engagement, stakeholder engagement, communications or a related field.
* Experience working alongside diverse communities and stakeholders.
* Strong relationship-building and communication skills.
* Highly organised, with excellent coordination and administrative capability.
* Comfortable managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
* A collaborative approach and willingness to learn from experienced practitioners.
* Culturally responsive, empathetic and thoughtful in the way you work.
* Comfortable travelling nationally when required.

Most importantly, you're someone who understands that great engagement isn't
transactional. You build trust, listen carefully, follow through on
commitments, and genuinely care about creating opportunities for people to have
their voices heard.

### Extra info

Part-time and flexible work arrangements can be considered. Think HQ is a
family-friendly, hybrid workplace.

Think HQ values inclusion and diversity. We welcome and actively encourage
applications from people of all ages, abilities and cultures, including
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, LGBTQIA+ people, people from
multicultural communities, people who speak a language other than English,
people with disabilities, older Australians and parents returning to the
workforce.

To support safe and ethical engagement with community participants,
appointment to this role is subject to holding (or obtaining) a valid Working
With Children Check and National Police Check.

Please include a cover letter so we can understand your experience and
motivation for joining Think HQ. Applications without a cover letter won't be
considered.
