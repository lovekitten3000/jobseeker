## Known conflicts between these documents

Recorded rather than silently resolved. See the evidence bank's
`## Open questions`.

- **Keeping It Real reach.** The conference speech says "over 130,000 accounts
  and nearly 400,000 views". The evaluation report says 398,322 paid reach,
  776,242 impressions, and 440,390 total reach across all digital platforms.
  The evaluation report is used, because it is the dated evaluation document
  and its figures are itemised. The speech may predate the final data.
- **Poster counts.** The report body reads "3042 posters", which looks like a
  tracked-changes artefact; the detailed metrics say 30 displayed and 320
  distributed overall. The 320 figure is used.
- **West Ball numbering.** The documents variously say West Ball 5, 6 and 56.
  West Ball 6, on 11 October 2025, is used, since the evaluation names that
  date.

## The attribution rule these documents make necessary

Both presentations are written in the first person plural: "our team's latest
campaign", "creation was led by our team". The Multicultural Health Conference
speech was co-delivered with the GWS Team Leader. These are team documents.

Never convert a team "we" into an individual "I" on a variant or an
application. Where the user's personal share of a piece of work is not stated
in a source, it is a `[GAP]` and the user is asked. This repo has already made
that error once, on ev:0004.
